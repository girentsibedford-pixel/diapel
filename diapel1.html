A few helpful answers

Frequently asked questions.

How do I choose a shoe size?

Check the size guide on each product and compare it with a pair that fits you well. If you are between sizes, review the product notes or contact our support team before ordering.

How much does shipping cost?

This demo shows sample USD rates at checkout: United States standard is $8 (free for item subtotals of $100 or more) or express for $15; Canada and the United Kingdom standard is $18; Ghana and Nigeria standard is $12. Estimated delivery is 3–5 business days for US standard, 1–2 for US express, and 5–10 for the other listed destinations. No real delivery is arranged.

Can I return an item?

Eligible unworn shoes and unopened socks may be returned within 30 days of delivery. See our for details and exceptions.

How can I track my delivery?

In a live store, a dispatch confirmation would include your tracking details. The order form in this demo does not create or ship a real order.

Are payments processed on this website?

No. This is a front-end demo and it does not accept or store card information or process payments. A real store must connect a trusted payment provider on a secure server.

How do discounts work?

Enter an available code in the discount field at checkout. STEP15 is a sample welcome offer shown on the home page; discounts in this demo are calculated locally and are not redeemable purchases.

Shopping with confidence

Returns & refunds.

Our sample policy is written to show customers how a real store could explain returns. Confirm the final terms with your business before publishing.

Return window

Request a return within 30 days after delivery. Shoes should be unworn, in their original packaging, and accompanied by proof of purchase. For hygiene, socks must be unopened and unused.

How to request a return

Contact customer care with your order number and the item you wish to return. We will provide return instructions after confirming eligibility. Do not send items to an address before receiving instructions.

Refunds

Once an eligible return is received and inspected, a refund is issued to the original payment method by the payment provider. Processing time depends on your bank or card issuer. Original shipping charges may not be refundable where permitted by law.

Damaged or incorrect items

Contact customer care as soon as possible with your order number and clear photos so the issue can be reviewed and resolved.

Exceptions and consumer rights

Personalized or final-sale products may have different return conditions where permitted. This policy does not limit any rights you have under applicable consumer-protection laws.

From our door to yours

Shipping & delivery.

This sample policy explains the information a customer should see before completing a real purchase. Delivery availability, costs, and timeframes must be configured for your actual service areas.

Delivery options

This demo displays sample USD rates: United States standard delivery costs $8, or is free when the item subtotal is at least $100; US express delivery costs $15. Standard delivery costs $18 to Canada and the United Kingdom, and $12 to Ghana and Nigeria. Estimated delivery is 3–5 business days for US standard, 1–2 for US express, and 5–10 for other listed destinations. These are examples only: no real shipment or tracking is provided.

Order processing

Orders are prepared after payment authorization. Weekends, public holidays, inventory checks, and peak periods may affect processing time. Customers should receive an order confirmation and a separate dispatch notice.

Delivery address

Please check that the recipient name, street, city, postal code, and contact details are accurate. Changes may not be possible after an order has been dispatched.

Tracking and delivery problems

Where available, tracking details should be sent with the dispatch notice. If a package is delayed, marked delivered but not received, or arrives damaged, contact customer care with your order number.

Taxes and import charges

Any applicable taxes or customs charges should be disclosed at checkout or by the relevant authority. Customers are responsible only for charges clearly stated before purchase and permitted by local law.
